# Obsidian2Typst v0.13.0

**Release date:** (draft; not released — waits for the manual check M25 and
the user's go)
**Minimum Obsidian version:** 1.5.0 (desktop only)
**Tests:** to be filled in from the release gate.

Entry layouts, second round (epic #139): the text wraps around the
statblock, landscape illustrations sit directly under the text and grow into
the free space, portrait illustrations use the larger of two forms, and the
quick reference gets its own running header, a labelled box column, section
rows and links.

---

## The text wraps around the statblock (US-030 AC-16a, #140)

- The entry's text fills the column beside the statblock until the
  statblock ends, then runs at full width; a paragraph that reaches the
  statblock's lower edge is split there at a word. Sub-headings go beside
  the statblock only with two lines of what follows; pictures, tables,
  lists, quotes and callouts only when they fit whole.
- Fixed on the way: a first paragraph taller than the page lost its text
  below the page's foot, without a warning.
- The same wrap for nested entries and entries outside an entries chapter.

## Landscape illustrations under the text, grown (US-031 AC-7a, #141)

- In `spread-landscape` and `page-landscape` the illustration follows the
  text and is enlarged into the space below it; a picture that reaches the
  text width first is centred in that space. When the space is smaller than
  the usual slot, the illustration moves to the next page as before.
- `ENTRY_SPREAD_EMPTY` counts the space below a centred picture.

## Portrait illustrations: the larger form wins (US-031 AC-7b, #142)

- `spread-portrait` and `page-portrait` lay the remaining text out in
  columns or stacked (text above, picture below, centred) and use the form
  with the larger picture; the stacked form counts only when its picture is
  at least as large as the column picture at `entries.image.minScale`. With
  no text left, the picture fills the page, centred.
- `layout: auto` places a portrait entry on one page when either form fits.

## Quick reference (US-033 AC-7, AC-9, #143, #144)

- Its pages, and the encounter sheet page, show their own title in the
  running header at every `page.runningHeaderLevel` (before, the last
  entry's or the chapter's name could show).
- The box column is headed with the stats label of the box key, or the key
  with a capital first letter (`Vigour`, not `vigour`).
- Section rows replace the Section column and repeat after a page break;
  entry names link to their entries.

## Changes to existing output

Books with entries chapters are laid out differently (the wrap, the
illustration places); notes without entries are unchanged.
