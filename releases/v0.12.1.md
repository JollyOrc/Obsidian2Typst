# Obsidian2Typst v0.12.1

**Release date:** 2026-11-08
**Minimum Obsidian version:** 1.5.0 (desktop only)
**Tests:** 2361/2361 unit, plugin and tooling tests passing (1 `todo`
marker, as before); 600/600 integration tests passing (gate of 2026-11-08)

A fix for the statblock on screen: a full row of tick boxes no longer runs
past the statblock's right frame. The PDF was not affected.

---

## Fixed: the box row stays inside the frame (US-035 AC-12, #138)

- In the `o2t-entry` preview (reading view and live preview) and in
  statblocks drawn with `api.renderStatblock`, a row of 15 tick boxes ended
  about 0.7 em past the statblock's right frame line, at every width. The
  boxes sat on grid columns that could not shrink below their content, and
  the extra space between the groups of five came from margins on boxes 5
  and 10.
- The boxes now sit on columns that shrink with the statblock, in three
  groups of five with a narrow empty column between the groups (the gap
  looks as before). The last box of a full row ends at the frame's inner
  edge at any width down to a very narrow pane (about 190 px at 16 px
  text), where the label and the gaps no longer fit.
- A CSS snippet that applied these rules by hand can be removed.

## Also in this release

- The preset details (Details view) list five fields of the entries layout
  that were missing: `entries.fullImageHeader`, `entries.image.minScale`,
  `entries.emptyWarn`, `entries.quickReference`,
  `entries.quickReferenceTitle`.

## Breaking changes and migration

None. `data.json` and preset notes keep their format; exports are
unchanged.
