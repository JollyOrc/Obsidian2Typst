# Obsidian2Typst v0.11.1

**Release date:** 2026-11-03
**Minimum Obsidian version:** 1.5.0 (desktop only)
**Tests:** 2162/2162 unit, plugin and tooling tests passing (1 `todo`
marker, as before); 506/506 integration tests passing (1 skipped without the
Typst CLI)

A fix for long books with image fit: the back notes and the empty pages
before them could print page number `1` and the last chapter's running
header. The export now compiles until the layout settles.

---

## Fixed: a long book with image fit settles (US-028, #116)

- With image fit on (`images.minScale` below 100), each image's choice
  (full size, smaller, floated, moved) moves everything after it. In a long
  book Typst did not finish placing the images in one compile, and pages
  that appear only in its last pass — the padding before the back notes and
  the back notes themselves — kept stale values: page number `1` and the
  header of the last chapter.
- The export now compiles the note again, starting from the choices of the
  compile before, until the layout settles — at most 8 compiles. The notice
  shows `Compiling… (2 of up to 8)`. A note that settles at once is compiled
  once, as before.
- If 8 compiles are not enough, the export compiles the note once more with
  image fit off (as `images.minScale: 100`) and reports `IMAGE_FIT_OFF`.
- The `.typ` in the export folder is always the one the PDF came from; it
  compiles to the same pages on its own.

## New warnings

- `LAYOUT_NOT_SETTLED` — Typst could not finish the layout; page numbers,
  running headers and image places may be wrong. Reported by the export
  when its last compile did not settle, and counted in the preview.
- `IMAGE_FIT_OFF` — image fit could not settle the layout in 8 compiles; the
  PDF was made without it (images at full size).
- Both are reported after the compile and never stop an export, also in
  strict mode.

## Changed behaviour

- The preview still compiles once, so for a long book with image fit it may
  differ from the export and count `LAYOUT_NOT_SETTLED`.
- The generated `.typ` has two new lines near the top, `#let o2t-fit-hints`
  and `#let o2t-fit-pinned`. A template that defines either name gets
  `TEMPLATE_NAME_TAKEN`, as for the plugin's other names.

## Breaking changes and migration

None. `data.json` and preset notes keep their format. A note without image
fit, or one that settles at once, gives the same PDF as before.
