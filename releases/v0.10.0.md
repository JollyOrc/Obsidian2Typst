# Obsidian2Typst v0.10.0

**Release date:** 2026-10-29
**Minimum Obsidian version:** 1.5.0 (desktop only)
**Tests:** 2031/2031 unit, plugin and tooling tests passing (1 `todo`
marker, as before); 480/480 integration tests passing (1 skipped without the
Typst CLI)

Book structure: four new features for printed books. Each is off by default,
so existing exports stay as they were, with one exception (see "Changed
behaviour").

---

## New: page breaks by heading level (US-025)

- `page.breakBefore` with the keys `h1` to `h6`, each `none` (built-in),
  `page`, `right` or `left` (`left` starts a new spread). A blank page is
  added when needed to reach the side; it prints its folio only.
- Where rules meet (consecutive headings, a heading that already starts a
  page) there is one break, and the strongest side counts.
- Six dropdown rows `Break before H1` to `Break before H6` in the settings tab
  and the style panel, each with a reset icon.

## New: book parts and signatures (US-026)

- The heading rules learn `colophon` and `back-notes`. The colophon goes on
  the first right-hand page after the cover; the back notes end on the very
  last page.
- `book.signature` (`0`, `4`, `8`, `16`, `32`) pads the book with blank pages
  before the back notes to a whole number of signatures.
- `book.romanFrontMatter` gives the front matter roman folios (i, ii, …), not
  printed on the cover and its back; the first chapter starts at 1.
- `book.firstChapter` (`left`, `right`) places the first chapter after the
  front matter.
- `book.colophonHeading` and `book.backNotesHeading` (`hidden`, built-in, or
  `printed`).
- New warnings `BOOK_PART_JOINED` (several parts of one kind are joined) and
  `BOOK_PART_EMPTY` (an empty hidden part is left out).
- A group `Book` of rows in the settings tab and the style panel.

## New: table of contents (US-027)

- `toc.depth` 1 to 6 turns on a table of contents (0 or unset: none, as
  before), with `toc.title` (built-in `Contents`), `toc.pageNumbers` and
  `toc.dotLeaders`. The entries link in the PDF; the contents pages have no
  running header.
- Its place: by default after the colophon; or before or after an H1, picked
  with the editor menu items `Table of contents before this heading` / `after
  this heading` (and `Remove table of contents placement`). Each asks before
  it changes the active preset note.
- Glossary terms, headings in callouts and unnumbered headings are not
  listed.
- A group `Contents` of rows and a `Contents place` line in the details
  pop-up.

## New: images that stay with their text (US-028)

- `images.minScale` (10 to 100 %, built-in 100 = off). An image that does not
  fit after its text shrinks down to that size; if it still does not fit, on
  a left page (or with facing pages off) it floats to the top of the next
  page and the text after it fills the gap; on a right page it moves to the
  next page and the gap stays.
- Inside callouts, quotes and list items, in the back notes and before a page
  break an image only shrinks or moves, never floats.
- Warnings after the compile, in the export report and the preview's count:
  `IMAGE_FLOATED` and `IMAGE_MOVED`. Shrinking is silent. Strict mode reports
  them but never stops on them.
- A row `Smallest image size` in a group `Images`.

## Changed behaviour

- A note with a top-level heading `Colophon` or `Back notes` now exports
  these sections as book parts (US-026): the colophon moves to the first
  right-hand page after the cover, the back notes to the end, and both
  headings are hidden by default.

## Fixed (found during this release's manual checks)

- With `images.minScale` below 100 an image no longer overprints the last
  text line above it, and a captioned image is centred as without the rule
  (TICKET-095).
- A floated image lets the text after it fill the gap again (TICKET-096).
- A floated image is never placed above its own text on its page
  (TICKET-097).

## Breaking changes and migration

- To keep the old output for a note with a `Colophon` or `Back notes`
  heading, add to the preset:

  ```yaml
  headingRules:
    Colophon: none
    Back notes: none
  ```

No other migration. Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` into
`<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/obsidian2typst/`.

## Known limitations

- During testing a Typst panic (`unreachable`) was seen after about nine
  whole-book compiles in one compiler instance; each of those compiles
  succeeds in a fresh process. Not verified (backlog row "Compiler
  stability").
- A float that fills the next page's top so that the paragraph after it no
  longer fits leaves the gap; it is still reported `IMAGE_FLOATED` (US-028
  AC-8).
