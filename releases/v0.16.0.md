# Obsidian2Typst v0.16.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-18
**Minimum Obsidian version:** 1.5.0 (desktop only)
**Tests:** 2841 unit, plugin and tooling tests passed, 1 `todo`; 692/692 integration tests passing (working tree; the clean-clone gate of the release build is recorded in the project's state files)

Exports follow the preferred UI standard for Obsidian plugins: an export dialog when you want to choose, a direct
export when the folder is known, a progress tab for long exports that you can cancel, and clearer notices.

---

## New features

- **The export dialog** (`Export…`: a new command, and the first item of the ribbon menu). Title `Export <note>`;
  the preset as a dropdown (grouped as in the ribbon menu; invalid presets are not offered); the export folder as a
  full-width field with `Choose folder…` and `Export` beside it; a red line when the folder is refused (empty, or
  not an absolute path) and a grey line saying what happens (`Book.pdf will be written.` or `Book.pdf exists (from
  2026-11-17 14:02) and will be replaced.`); a gear `More options` that opens the plugin's settings. Choosing a
  preset fills in its remembered folder. **Enter** exports; **Escape**, the X or a click outside close the dialog
  and save nothing. The folder and the preset are saved only when `Export` runs (the preset becomes the active one).
- **A progress tab for long exports.** An export still running after about 2 s opens a tab `Exporting <note>`
  beside your tabs, without taking the focus: the current step (`Converting`, `Compiling`, `Compiling (2 of up to
  8)`, `Writing files`, …), a bar, the warnings as they are found (code in bold, message, line, a yellow border), and
  **`Cancel`**. Cancel stops the compile at once; no PDF is written; files written before stay. At the end the tab
  says `Exported` (or `Export failed`, `Export cancelled`, `Export aborted (strict mode)`) and offers `Close`; the
  next long export reuses it. A short export opens no tab.
- **Copy debug info** (the bug icon next to the version in the settings tab): copies the plugin's and Obsidian's
  versions, your enabled plugins with their versions, and the plugin's settings file as plain text, for a bug
  report. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Reset a section to defaults:** *Page*, *Text* and *Book* in the settings tab end with `Reset this section to
  defaults` (after a question that starts on `Cancel`). It removes your Defaults values of that section only.

## Changed behaviour

- **`Export current note` and `Export to PDF` export at once** into the active preset's remembered folder, without a
  folder dialog. Without a remembered folder they open the export dialog.
- **Notices:** `✅ Exported Book.pdf to <folder>` (with warnings: `, 3 warnings (see export-warnings.md)`),
  `❌ Export cancelled.`, `❌ Export failed: <message>`, `❌ Export aborted: 3 warnings (strict mode)` followed by
  the first five. Notices stay about 5 s; refusals end with a full stop. The `Compiling…` notices give way to the
  progress tab.
- The settings tab's first line now holds the version and the bug icon; there is no heading with the plugin's name.
- All texts of the plugin's screens, menus and notices come from one string table. The wording is unchanged.

## Limits

- Tested on: Obsidian 1.15.1 on macOS (Apple silicon), in a scratch vault (manual check M33: every entry point with
  and without a remembered folder, the dialog's keys and lines, the progress tab on a 301-page book, Cancel during the
  compile, every notice, Copy debug info, the section reset; and M32 for the progress tab's behaviour). Not tested:
  Windows, Linux, mobile, other Obsidian versions; Enter while an input method composes text (no input method was at
  hand).
- The progress tab opens about 2 s into an export; with a fast machine and a short note you see the notice only.

## Breaking changes and migration

None. Settings and presets are read as before.
