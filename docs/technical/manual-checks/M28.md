# M28 — Text beside a statblock: the paragraph continues below without a gap (#160)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #164 (US-030 AC-16c, the amendment for #160), and after any
change to the wrap of the text around a statblock (`o2t-wrap`) or to the
portrait page layouts that place a picture after it.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop and a PDF viewer. Prepared by the developer
(SCAR-012): the test build installed in the scratch vault (`npm run
install:scratch`; `main.js`, `manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to
the fresh build; checksums in the hand-over note) and the note
`Wrap check.md` at the vault's root: a chapter `Menagerie` with three
entries, each with a statblock and a long opening text of invented words
(`a0 a1 …`, `b0 …`, `c0 …` and `d0 …`), each on its own page. Empty export
folders: `spikes/typst-wasm/m28-export/1` and `spikes/typst-wasm/m28-export/2`.
The vault's
state before the install is backed up with a checksum list in the
developer's record. Part B uses your own vault; nothing there is prepared or
changed by the developer.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`) for part A,
and in your own vault for part B. **Expected version:** `0.14.1-m28.1`
(first line of the settings tab, also the plugin list). If it differs, stop:
the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian once so the new build is
loaded.

**Known limitations (SCAR-024, SCAR-032, SCAR-045):** the developer measured
the fixture in Node with the plugin's conversion and the bundled compiler,
with the sample's `Entries` preset, plain and with a template that justifies
(2026-11-10): at every cut the next line followed at the same distance as the
lines of the paragraph. Not measured: Obsidian's own export, your preset
`A5 Print MU White` and its template (part B is that measurement). The column
beside the statblock may set its lines a hair closer together or further
apart than the text below (at most a tenth of a line); that is the rule, not
a defect. The words are invented.

Read every page at reading size (100 %, SCAR-025) and look closely at the
lines just below each statblock.

## Steps

### A — the scratch vault

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.14.1-m28.1`. Close the settings.
2. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Entries`. Open `Wrap check.md` and
   run `O2T: Export to PDF` into `spikes/typst-wasm/m28-export/1`. Open the
   PDF.
3. On each of the three entry pages (`Fen Hare`, `Marsh Owl`, `Reed Cat`):
   the text beside the statblock ends level with the statblock's lower edge
   (its last line neither clearly above nor clearly below the frame), and
   the next line, at full width, follows at the same distance as any two
   lines of the paragraph — no gap of a line or more, no paragraph gap, the
   first full-width line not indented. Under `Reed Cat` the column also holds
   the heading `Habits` before the split paragraph.
4. Every word of each entry is there once, in order (spot-check the words on
   both sides of each cut: the last word beside the statblock and the first
   word below it are consecutive numbers).
5. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Entries justified` and export again,
   into `spikes/typst-wasm/m28-export/2`.
   Step 3 holds with justified lines (the column's last line set full to
   its right edge).

### B — your own vault (your preset `A5 Print MU White`)

6. Close Obsidian. In your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/obsidian2typst/`, keep a
   copy of the three files `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css`
   somewhere outside the vault. Copy the same three files from the scratch
   vault's `.obsidian/plugins/obsidian2typst/` into your vault's folder. Start
   Obsidian; the settings tab reads `Obsidian2Typst 0.14.1-m28.1`.
7. Export the chapter with the entry from your report (#160) with your
   preset `A5 Print MU White`. At that entry, and at two or three other
   entries whose text is split at the statblock, step 3 holds.
8. Close Obsidian and put your three saved files back (or keep the test
   build until the release; your choice). Report which you did.

9. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about — a
   line that overlaps the statblock, a column visibly squeezed, a page that
   broke differently from before? Report it even if it seems unrelated.

## Result

Record: date, the build `0.14.1-m28.1`, passed or the failing steps with
what was seen, and for part B the entries looked at and what you saw.
