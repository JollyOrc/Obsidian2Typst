# M35 — The filled name band in Obsidian, in print and on a card (US-044)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #187, #188 and #189, before the release 0.18.0; ticket #190. What the tests cannot show is checked
here (SCAR-025, SCAR-045, SCAR-046): the band in Obsidian's reading view, the printed page at reading size, and a
Game Card Creator creature card drawn through the installed plugin.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop and a printer. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012, SCAR-018) in the scratch vault
(`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`, opened with `Open folder as vault`):

- the **test build `0.18.0-m35.1`** of this plugin, installed by `npm run install:scratch` and enabled;
- **Game Card Creator 0.7.0** (the released code), installed and enabled, with one kind rule `M35/Creatures` →
  `creature`, the output folder `spikes/m35/cards` outside the vault, and *Equal title bands* on for creatures;
- the preset **`M35 Band`** (the `Entries` preset with an umber band: `bandFill: "#8A4A2A"`, `bandColor:
  "#F3EEE3"`, frame and rules umber, fill `#E3D3B0`), set as the active preset;
- two invented creatures in `M35/Creatures/`: `Reed Stalker.md` (one-line name) and `Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey
  Reaches.md` (a name that wraps), each with its properties and an `o2t-entry` block; the note `M35/Menagerie.md`
  with both entries under *Menagerie*, each followed by text that wraps around its statblock;
- the starting state: `spikes/m35/m35-before.txt` (315 files) and a full copy in `spikes/m35/vault-before/`, made
  while the scratch vault was not open.

**Run in the scratch vault**. **Expected version:** `0.18.0` (first line of this plugin's settings tab). If
it differs, stop.

**Decided before the run (SCAR-032):** each step names what is expected; the check passes when every step shows
it. A difference is written down with its step number and nothing is changed during the run; afterwards each one
is a bug (fixed and re-checked before the release) or a point the owner decides. A step that cannot be done is
recorded as not run.

## Steps

1. Settings → Community plugins: **Obsidian2Typst 0.18.0-m35.1** and **Game Card Creator 0.7.0**, both on. In
   Obsidian2Typst's settings, the active preset is `M35 Band`; its Details list shows `entries.statblock.bandFill`
   `#8A4A2A` and `entries.statblock.bandColor` `#F3EEE3`. Close the settings.
2. Open `M35/Creatures/Reed Stalker.md` in reading view. Expected: the statblock's name band is **umber** to the frame
   on the left, top and right (no paper strip), `◆ Reed Stalker ◆` in light parchment, and **no line** under the band;
   every other line of the statblock is there.
3. Open `M35/Creatures/Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey Reaches.md`. Expected: the name on two lines inside **one**
   umber band, the ◆ marks level with the middle of the two lines. Make the pane narrow and wide again: the band
   follows, nothing overlaps.
4. With `M35/Menagerie.md` open: ribbon icon `Obsidian2Typst` → **`Export…`** with `M35 Band`. Open the PDF: both
   statblocks have the band as in steps 2 and 3, and the text beside each statblock wraps around it.
5. Print the page with the dragon at **100 %**. At arm's length: the band's edges meet the frame, the name is easy
   to read, the VIGOUR boxes and STATE cells below it are clear of the band.
6. Grid icon **Create cards…** in the left ribbon; tick the two creatures in `M35/Creatures`, click **Create
   cards**. Open the PDF in `spikes/m35/cards`:
   both cards show the umber band; with *Equal title bands* both names have **the same size**. If the dragon's
   name fits on one line, both bands are the same height; if it wraps (with a `CREATURE_TITLE_WRAPPED` warning),
   only its band is taller and the other keeps its height (Game Card Creator's rule for creature cards, its manual
   *Equal title bars*). *Corrected 2026-11-26 after the run: the first text said both bands then take the wrapped
   height, which contradicts Game Card Creator's documented rule (SCAR-024).*
7. Anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report it even if it seems unrelated.

## Record

Date, the build, passed or the differences by step. The developer then compares the disk with what the steps
wrote (the PDFs, the `.typ`, the warnings files, both plugins' `data.json`) and undoes the vault from the full copy
(`shasum -c m35-before.txt`).

## Result

**Passed 2026-11-26** (the owner: "most tests pass" — steps 1 to 5 and 7 as written; step 6 differed from the first
text, which was wrong, and passed against the corrected text: the owner chose "a) is fine"). Build `0.18.0-m35.1`,
Game Card Creator 0.7.0. Read from the disk afterwards (SCAR-015): the card run used *Smallest title size* **11**
(changed in the settings tab during the run; prepared was the default 7), so the dragon's name could not fit on one
line: both names at 11 pt, the dragon's band two lines and the Reed Stalker's one, warning `CREATURE_TITLE_WRAPPED`
`1 of 2 titles wrap at 11 pt` — Game Card Creator's documented behaviour, the same without a fill. Fixture fault
(mine): the dragon's `vigour` 30 is not the sum 8+5+4 of the preset's `sum`, so both the export and the card run
warn `STAT_SUM_MISMATCH`; no effect on the band. The export's warnings: `ENTRY_AUTO` ×2 and that one. The export PDF
went to the Desktop (the preset's last export folder), not the prepared folder; nothing depends on the place. The
run also made `Untitled.base` and `Untitled.canvas` in the vault root (38 and 2 bytes, not part of the check). Run
files kept in `spikes/m35/after-run/`.
