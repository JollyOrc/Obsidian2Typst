# M15 — Page breaks by heading level (US-025 AC-2, AC-5 to AC-7, AC-10)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after TICKET-075 and TICKET-076 (page breaks in the core, the
dropdown rows), and after any change to the break lines, the blank-page test
of the header or the rows.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): the test
build is installed in the scratch vault (`npm run install:scratch`; `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the fresh build, `main.js`
sha256 `a5c96785fdaf…`), with the generated sample vault as `Sample/` (73
files). The new preset note `Obsidian2Typst/presets/Breaks.md` sets
`page.breakBefore` to `h1: right`, `h2: page` and nothing else; `Breaks.md`
exists once in the vault (listing, 2026-10-28). The plugin data
(`data.json`: active preset `A5 MU Print`; Defaults with folios and the running
header on at level 4, body 12 pt, headings 18 pt) and every preset note are
backed up in `spikes/typst-wasm/m15-state-backup/before/` with checksums. Empty
export folders `spikes/typst-wasm/m15-export/1` and `2` are in the project
folder.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.10.0` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian once
so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations:** the sample note is filler text; its 149 warnings are
its own link cases on purpose and the missing task-box glyph (as in M14). The rows have no group heading of
their own: they follow `Link page word`.

The pages below were measured from `Sample/Book.md` with the preset `Breaks`
and the vault's Defaults, through the plugin's merge, the pipeline and the
bundled compiler (2026-10-28, scratch probe `m15/measure.test.ts`). Measured
again on 2026-10-29 through the plugin's `convertNote` with the font catalog,
after the sample note gained two chapters at its end (TICKET-081).

## Steps

Keep the settings tab and, later, the style panel open from step 1 to step 5
(SCAR-016).

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.10.0`. In the Defaults, after `Link page word`, there are six rows
   `Break before H1` to `Break before H6`. Each is a dropdown that shows `—`
   and offers `—`, `none`, `page`, `right`, `left`, with a reset icon.
2. Set `Break before H3` to `left`. Without closing the tab, click its reset
   icon. The question is titled `Reset "Break before H3"?` and says `The value
   "left" is removed from the Defaults. …`. Press Escape: the row still shows
   `left`. Click the icon again and confirm: the row shows `—`.
3. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Breaks`, open `Sample/Book.md` and
   open the style panel (preview). In its Page rows, `Break before H1` shows
   `right` and `Break before H2` shows `page`, both greyed out with `Set by
   preset "Breaks"; change it in the preset note`, without a reset icon;
   `Break before H3` can be changed.
4. In the preview, type `440` in the page range field and press Enter: it
   reads `The document has 439 pages`. Then use the ranges `1-4`, `51-54` and
   `428-439` to see the pages named here. Page 1 is the cover; page 2 is empty except
   its page number `1` at the bottom (no header); page 3 starts with `1
   Mollit`; page 4 starts with `1.1 Cras Ac` (a section on a new page; its
   header reads `Mollit`). The chapters start on pages 53, 117, 183, 231, 297,
   359, 429 (`8 Glossary`), 435 (`9 Appendix`), 437 (`10 Senectus Netus`)
   and 439 (`11 Pretium Fames`), always a right-hand page; the pages 52,
   116, 230, 358, 436 and 438 are empty except their page number (51, 115,
   229, 357, 435, 437), with no header. Look at page 2 and page 52 at reading size,
   and zoom into the top edge (no header text) and the page number (SCAR-025).
   In the glossary the terms run on one after the other (no term starts a page
   because of the rules).
5. With the preview open, edit `Obsidian2Typst/presets/Breaks.md`: change
   `h2: page` to `h2: none` and save. The preview updates by itself: with the
   range `1-4`, `1.1 Cras Ac` now stands on page 3 under the text of `1
   Mollit`; the range `430` reads `The document has 429 pages`; the empty
   pages are 2, 222, 348, 418, 426 and 428. Change it back to `h2: page`: the
   range `440` reads `The document has 439 pages` again.
6. Run `Obsidian2Typst: Export current note` into `m15-export/1`. `Book.pdf`
   has 439 pages with the same pages as the preview in step 4;
   `export-warnings.md` lists 149 entries, as in M14 (corrected after the run:
   the text said 148, measured without the font catalog).
7. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).

After the check, the developer compares the preset notes and `data.json` with
`m15-state-backup/handover/` (checksums) and restores what differs; the preset
`Breaks.md` and the export folders stay unless the user asks to remove them.
