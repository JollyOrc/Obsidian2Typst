# M18 — Images that stay with their text (US-028 AC-4 to AC-6, AC-9, AC-11, AC-13)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after TICKET-089 to TICKET-093, TICKET-095 to TICKET-097 (the field,
the lines, the helper, the decisions read back, the row, the look, floats,
a float never on its own page), and after any change to the image fit.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): the test
build is installed in the scratch vault (`npm run install:scratch`; `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the fresh build, `main.js`
sha256 `787b899cdbea…`), with the sample vault as `Sample/` (73 files, equal
to `tests/fixtures/sample/`). Two new preset notes (group `Print`):
`Obsidian2Typst/presets/Image fit.md` holds the settings of `Contents` (M17)
and `images: minScale: 70`; `Image fit single.md` is the same with
`facingPages: false`. A new note `Image fit check.md` at the vault root: 19
filler paragraphs and four images `![[map.png|400]]` (lines 13, 41, 53, and
line 62 in a `[!note]` callout), placed so that the four decisions happen; it
has no conversion warning. Each of the three names exists once in the vault
(listing, 2026-10-29). The plugin data (`data.json`: active preset `Book
parts`, strict mode off, no image settings in the Defaults), every preset note
and `Sample/` are backed up in `spikes/typst-wasm/m18-state-backup/before/`
(93 files) and, as handed over, in `handover/` (96 files), with checksums. Two
empty export folders `spikes/typst-wasm/m18-export/1` and `2` are in the
project folder.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.10.0` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian once
so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations:** the sample note is filler text; its 149 warnings are
its own link cases on purpose and the missing task-box glyph (as in M14 to
M17). The images of `Sample/Book.md` are small: with facing pages every one
fits (the output equals M17's), so the floats and the shrink are shown
single-sided, and a move only in the check note. The check note is tuned to
the preset `Image fit`: one paragraph more or less before an image changes its
decision. It has no headings, so its contents page (p. 1) shows only the title
`Contents`, and its last page is blank (the signature of 8).

The pages below were measured from the files as installed (the two preset
notes, `Image fit check.md`, `data.json`), through the plugin's `convertNote`
(preset and Defaults, the font catalog), the bundled compiler and the PDF
reader (2026-10-29, scratch probe `m094b/final/m18.test.ts`; first checked
against M17: `Contents` still gives 424 pages and 149 warnings). Pages 12, 68,
175, 176 and 234 of `Book.md` and every page of the check note were looked at
at reading size.

## Steps

Keep the settings tab and, later, the style panel and the preview open from
step 1 to step 6 (SCAR-016).

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.10.0`. In the Defaults, after `Contents dot leaders`, there is the
   row `Smallest image size`, a number field with the hint `%, 10 to 100` and
   a reset icon; the field is empty. Type `90` and leave the field. Click its
   reset icon: the question reads `The value "90" is removed from the
   Defaults. Presets that do not set this field use the built-in value.`
   Confirm: the field is empty again.
2. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Image fit` (under `Print`), open
   `Sample/Book.md` and open the style panel (preview). In its rows,
   `Smallest image size` shows `70`, greyed out with `Set by preset "Image
   fit"; change it in the preset note`, without a reset icon. Click the
   panel's `info` icon: the pop-up lists `Smallest image size` with `70` from
   the preset. Close it. The status line reads `Preview of: Book · 149
   warnings`; the range `425` reads `The document has 424 pages` (as M17).
   Use the range `12` (number `7`): the striped image with the caption
   `Pellentesque laborum netus` is centred, with space above it, the caption
   centred under it, nothing printed over the line above (look at reading
   size and zoom into the image's top edge, SCAR-025).
3. In the ribbon menu choose `Image fit single`. The preview updates by
   itself: the status line reads `Preview of: Book · 153 warnings`, the range
   `417` reads `The document has 416 pages`. Then:
   - range `68` (number `64`): the striped image stands at the bottom, after
     the paragraph ending `excepteur nisi?`, not at the top of the page;
   - range `175-176`: page 175 (number `171`) ends with `… anim ut cillum
     ut.`; page 176 (number `172`) starts with a small striped image at the
     top, above `4.1.1.2 Malesuada Praesent Ultricies` (a float; a small gap
     stays at the bottom of page 175);
   - range `234` (number `230`): the last item of the page is a small striped
     image right after the paragraph ending `… est officia.` (shrunk; at full
     size it would go to the next page).
4. Run `Obsidian2Typst: Export current note` into `m18-export/1`. The notice
   names 153 warnings. `Book.pdf` has 416 pages, as the preview.
   `export-warnings.md` lists 153 entries: the 149 of M17 and four
   `IMAGE_FLOATED` at lines 3035, 3655, 5572 and 5675, e.g. `Image
   "assets/figure-28.png" did not fit after its text; placed at the top of the
   next page`; no `IMAGE_MOVED`.
5. In the ribbon menu choose `Image fit` and open `Image fit check.md`. The
   preview shows 8 pages and the status line `Preview of: Image fit check ·
   3 warnings`:
   - page 2 (number `1`): `IMAGE 1 follows.`, then the image a little smaller
     than the others, at the bottom of the page (shrunk);
   - page 4 (number `3`): `IMAGE 2 follows.`, then directly `AFTER IMAGE 2.
     …` and the start of `Paragraph 16: …` fill the rest of the page; page 5
     (number `4`) starts with the image (floated, the text after it filled
     the gap);
   - page 5 ends `IMAGE 3 follows.` with an empty space below; page 6
     (number `5`) starts with the image, then `AFTER IMAGE 3. …` (moved);
   - page 6 ends with the callout's title `A map in a callout` in its box;
     page 7 (number `6`) goes on with the box holding the image, then `AFTER
     IMAGE 4. …` (moved in the callout; the box breaks, as without the rule).
6. In the settings tab switch `Strict mode` on. In the panel click
   `Refresh`: the preview still shows the 8 pages and `3 warnings` (the fit
   warnings never stop it). Export into `m18-export/2`: the notice names 3
   warnings and `Image fit check.pdf` is written (8 pages);
   `export-warnings.md` lists `IMAGE_FLOATED` at line 41 and `IMAGE_MOVED` at
   lines 53 and 62, with the texts `… placed at the top of the next page` and
   `… moved to the next page, the space before it stays empty`. Switch
   `Strict mode` off again.
7. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).

After the check, the developer compares the preset notes, `data.json` and
the notes with `m18-state-backup/handover/` (checksums) and restores what
differs; the two presets, the check note and the export folders stay unless
the user asks to remove them.
