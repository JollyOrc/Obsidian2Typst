# M16 — Book parts and signatures (US-026 AC-3 to AC-6, AC-9, AC-10)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after TICKET-078 to TICKET-081 (book parts and pages in the core,
the Book rows, the sample vault's colophon and back notes), and after any
change to the part, padding or folio lines or the Book rows.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): the test
build is installed in the scratch vault (`npm run install:scratch`; `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the fresh build, `main.js`
sha256 `77c39c19129c…`), with the regenerated sample vault as `Sample/` (73
files; `Sample/Book.md` ends with `# Senectus Netus` and `# Pretium Fames`).
The new preset note `Obsidian2Typst/presets/Book parts.md` (group `Print`)
sets A5, folios, the running header at level 2, the cover `auto`, `book:
signature: 8`, `romanFrontMatter: true`, `firstChapter: left`, and
`headingRules` `Senectus Netus: colophon`, `Pretium Fames: back-notes`;
`Book parts.md` exists once in the vault (listing, 2026-10-29). The plugin
data (`data.json`: active preset `Breaks`; Defaults with folios and the
running header on at level 4, body 12 pt, headings 18 pt), every preset note
and the old `Sample/` are backed up in
`spikes/typst-wasm/m16-state-backup/before/` with checksums. An empty export
folder `spikes/typst-wasm/m16-export/1` is in the project folder.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.10.0` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian once
so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations:** the sample note is filler text; its 149 warnings are
its own link cases on purpose and the missing task-box glyph (as in M14). The
colophon and back-notes headings are hidden (the built-in), so neither
heading is printed. There is no table of contents yet (US-027). The Book rows
have no group heading of their own: they follow `Break before H6`.

The pages below were measured from `Sample/Book.md` with the preset `Book
parts` and the vault's Defaults, through the plugin's `convertNote` (preset
and Defaults, the font catalog), the bundled compiler and the PDF reader
(2026-10-29, scratch probe `m082/measure.test.ts`); pages 2, 3, 4, 422 and 424
were looked at at reading size, their page numbers zoomed in.

## Steps

Keep the settings tab and, later, the style panel open from step 1 to step 5
(SCAR-016).

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.10.0`. In the Defaults, after `Break before H6`, there are five
   rows, each with a reset icon: `Pages per signature` (a dropdown offering
   `—`, `0`, `4`, `8`, `16`, `32`), `Roman folios in the front matter` (a
   switch), `First chapter` (`—`, `left`, `right`), `Colophon heading` and
   `Back notes heading` (each `—`, `hidden`, `printed`).
2. Set `Pages per signature` to `4`, then to `32`. Without closing the tab,
   click its reset icon. The question is titled `Reset "Pages per
   signature"?` and says `The value "32" is removed from the Defaults. …`.
   Press Escape: the row still shows `32`. Click the icon again and confirm:
   the row shows `—`.
3. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Book parts` (under `Print`), open
   `Sample/Book.md` and open the style panel (preview). In its rows, `Pages
   per signature` shows `8`, `Roman folios in the front matter` is on and
   `First chapter` shows `left`, each greyed out with `Set by preset "Book
   parts"; change it in the preset note`, without a reset icon; `Colophon
   heading` and `Back notes heading` can be changed.
4. In the preview, type `425` in the page range field and press Enter: it
   reads `The document has 424 pages` (53 signatures of 8). Then use the
   ranges `1-5` and `420-424`:
   - page 1 is the cover picture alone, without a number; page 2 is empty,
     with no number and no header;
   - page 3 is the colophon: `Scelerisque pretium sint aliquip …` and
     `Dictum turpis feugiat …`, no heading above it, no header, the number
     `iii` at the bottom right;
   - page 4, a left-hand page, starts with `1 Mollit` and has the number `1`
     at the bottom left; page 5 has the header `Cras Ac` and the number `2`;
   - page 421 (number 418) is the last page of the `Appendix`; pages 422 and
     423 are empty except their numbers `419` and `420` at the bottom left
     and right, with no header;
   - page 424, the last, holds the back notes: `Ultricies nullam rutrum
     blandit …`, no heading above it, no header, the number `421` at the
     bottom left.
   Look at pages 3 and 422 at reading size and zoom into the top edge (no
   header text) and the page number (SCAR-025).
5. With the preview open, edit `Obsidian2Typst/presets/Book parts.md`: change
   `signature: 8` to `signature: 16` and save. The preview updates by itself:
   the range `433` reads `The document has 432 pages`; pages 422 to 431 are
   empty except their numbers (419 to 428); page 432 holds the back notes
   (number 429). Change it back to `signature: 8`: the range `425` reads `The
   document has 424 pages` again.
6. Run `Obsidian2Typst: Export current note` into `m16-export/1`. `Book.pdf`
   has 424 pages with the same pages as the preview in step 4; the PDF
   viewer's sidebar or bookmarks have no entry `Senectus Netus` or `Pretium
   Fames`; `export-warnings.md` lists 149 entries, as in M14, and none starts
   with `BOOK_PART`.
7. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).

After the check, the developer compares the preset notes and `data.json` with
`m16-state-backup/handover/` (checksums) and restores what differs; the preset
`Book parts.md` and the export folder stay unless the user asks to remove
them.
