# M23 — The encounter sheet printed at 100 % (US-036)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #135 and #136, and after any change to the encounter sheet
or the statblock's boxes and cells.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop, a printer for A4 and A5 at 100 %, a pencil.
Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): as M21. Uses the export of M21
(`spikes/typst-wasm/m21-export/1`); if M21 was not run, export
`Sample/Menagerie.md` with the preset `Entries` there first.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.14.0` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed.

**Known limitations:** the sample preset sets no `rule` and no `credit`, so
neither is printed; the automated tests cover both.

## Steps

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.14.0`. Close the settings.
2. Open `Menagerie - Encounter Sheet.pdf` from the M21 export: one A4
   portrait page. At the top `Encounter Sheet` (no `Round` line); below it
   the key: each icon with its label, `Fine Hurt Down Fading Gone`.
3. Below: 14 framed rows, two across, seven down. Each row: `NAME` and a
   line, then `MIGHT`, `WITS`, `SPEED` each with a small box; 30 tick boxes
   in two rows of 15 (groups of five) with the five icon cells beside them;
   three lines `ATTACK ____ DICE ___` one above the other. Every row is
   blank.
4. Print it on A4 at 100 % ("actual size"). Measure a row: about 91 mm wide;
   a tick box about 2.6 mm. Mark a few boxes and a cell with a pencil: each
   mark stays inside its box (SCAR-025).
5. In `Menagerie.pdf`, page 39 is the sheet page of `Menagerie` (bookmark
   `Encounter Sheet` under `Menagerie`): the heading, the key, and 4 rows in
   one column, each with three attack slots (two side by side, one below),
   on one page. Page 45 is the sheet page of `Relics`. Print page 39 on A5
   at 100 %: a tick box about 3.2 mm.
6. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).

## Record

Append a dated line to `docs/technical/spike-typst-wasm.md`, section "Manual
runs": date, build version and checksum, Obsidian version and operating system,
result per step, and keep the marked print with the record (a photo is
enough).
