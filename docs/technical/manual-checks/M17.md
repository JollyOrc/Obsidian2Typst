# M17 — Table of contents (US-027 AC-2 to AC-8)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after TICKET-083 to TICKET-087 (the contents fields, lines, place
and pages in the core, the editor menu items, the Contents rows), and after
any change to the contents lines, their place or pages, the menu items or
the Contents rows.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): the test
build is installed in the scratch vault (`npm run install:scratch`; `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the fresh build, `main.js`
sha256 `d9216accd333…`), with the sample vault as `Sample/` (73 files, equal
to `tests/fixtures/sample/`). The new preset note
`Obsidian2Typst/presets/Contents.md` (group `Print`) holds the settings of
`Book parts` (M16: A5, folios, the running header at level 2, the cover
`auto`, signatures of 8, roman front matter, the first chapter on a left
page, `Senectus Netus` as colophon and `Pretium Fames` as back notes) and
`toc: depth: 2`; `Contents.md` exists once in the vault (listing,
2026-10-29). The plugin data (`data.json`: active preset `Book parts`, no
contents settings in the Defaults), every preset note and the old `Sample/`
are backed up in `spikes/typst-wasm/m17-state-backup/before/` (92 files) with
checksums. An empty export folder `spikes/typst-wasm/m17-export/1` is in the
project folder.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.10.0` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian once
so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations:** the sample note is filler text; its 149 warnings are
its own link cases on purpose and the missing task-box glyph (as in M14 and
M16). `Sample/Book.md` has no heading inside a callout, quote or list item,
so the bookmarks of such headings (AC-3) are shown by the automated PDF tests
only. The colophon and back-notes headings are hidden (the built-in), so they
are not listed. An empty `Contents title` in the settings tab means the
built-in `Contents`; no title is possible only in a preset note (`title:
''`, decision 7).

The pages below were measured from `Sample/Book.md` with the preset
`Contents` and the vault's Defaults, through the plugin's `convertNote`
(preset and Defaults, the font catalog), the bundled compiler and the PDF
reader (2026-10-29, scratch probe `m088/measure.test.ts`; first checked
against M16: `Book parts` still gives 424 pages and 149 warnings); pages 4,
5, 177, 178 and 180 were looked at at reading size.

## Steps

Keep the settings tab and, later, the style panel and the preview open from
step 1 to step 7 (SCAR-016).

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.10.0`. In the Defaults, after `Back notes heading`, there are four
   rows, each with a reset icon: `Contents depth` (a dropdown offering `—`,
   `0` to `6`), `Contents title` (an empty text field), `Contents page
   numbers` and `Contents dot leaders` (switches).
2. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Contents` (under `Print`), open
   `Sample/Book.md` and open the style panel (preview). In its rows,
   `Contents depth` shows `2`, greyed out with `Set by preset "Contents";
   change it in the preset note`, without a reset icon; `Contents title`,
   `Contents page numbers` and `Contents dot leaders` can be changed. Click
   the panel's `info` icon: the pop-up lists the four Contents rows and its
   last row reads `Contents place` — `default`. Close it.
3. In the preview, type `425` in the page range field and press Enter: it
   reads `The document has 424 pages`. Then use the range `1-7`:
   - page 3 is the colophon (`Scelerisque pretium sint aliquip …`), number
     `iii`, as in M16;
   - page 4 starts with the bold title `Contents`, then `1 Mollit` with dots
     to `1`, indented `1.1 Cras Ac … 1`, `1.2 Sapien Viverra … 14`, and so on
     to `7 Cras … 344`; the number `iv` at the bottom left, no header;
   - page 5 goes on with `7.1 Fusce Voluptate … 345` to `8 Glossary … 412`
     and `9 Appendix … 418`; the number `v` at the bottom right, no header;
   - page 6, a left-hand page, starts with `1 Mollit` and has the number `1`;
     page 7 has the header `Cras Ac` and the number `2`.

   The contents list 9 chapters and 26 sections; no glossary term (the terms
   under `8 Glossary`), no `Senectus Netus`, no `Pretium Fames`. Look at page
   4 at reading size and zoom into the dot leaders and the column of numbers
   (SCAR-025).
4. In the style panel type `Inhalt` into `Contents title` and leave the
   field; switch `Contents dot leaders` off. The preview updates by itself:
   page 4 starts with `Inhalt`, the entries have no dots, the numbers stand
   in a column at the right; still 424 pages, `1 Mollit` on page 6. Click the
   reset icon of each of the two rows and confirm: page 4 reads `Contents`
   with dots again.
5. In the note, click into line 1875 (`# Praesent`), then right-click it. The
   menu has `Use as glossary section`, `Table of contents before this
   heading` and `Table of contents after this heading`. Close the menu
   (Escape). Do the same on line 1884 (`## Mi Ac`) and on line 7235 (`#
   Senectus Netus`): no `Table of contents` item. Now right-click line 1875
   again and choose `Table of contents after this heading`, with the keyboard
   only from here: a dialog titled `Change preset "Contents"` reads `Place
   the table of contents of preset "Contents" after "Praesent"?` with
   `Change preset` and `Cancel`; `Cancel` has the focus. Press Escape: the
   dialog closes and `Contents.md` is unchanged. Choose the item again and
   click `Change preset`: the notice `Preset "Contents" changed`; in
   `Contents.md` the `toc` group now reads `depth: 2`, `heading: Praesent`,
   `position: after` (the rest unchanged).
6. The preview updates by itself: the range `425` still reads `The document
   has 424 pages`; in the range `1-6` page 4 is now `1 Mollit` (number `1`),
   there is no contents page in the front. In the range `176-181`:
   - page 177 ends the `Praesent` chapter (header `Laborum Mauris`, number
     `174`);
   - pages 178 and 179 are the contents, numbers `175` and `176`, no header;
     `4 Dui Velit … 177`, `4.1 Elit Amet … 177`, `9 Appendix … 420`;
   - page 180 starts with `4 Dui Velit`, number `177`.

   Click the `info` icon: `Contents place` reads `after "Praesent"`
   (preset). Close it.
7. Right-click line 1875 again: the menu has `Table of contents before this
   heading` and `Remove table of contents placement`. Choose `Remove table of
   contents placement` and click `Change preset` in its question (`Move the
   table of contents of preset "Contents" back to the default place?`). The
   `toc` group of `Contents.md` reads `depth: 2` again; the preview shows the
   pages of step 3 again.
8. Run `Obsidian2Typst: Export current note` into `m17-export/1`. `Book.pdf`
   has 424 pages, the same as the preview in step 3. In the PDF viewer, click
   `3 Praesent` in the contents: it goes to page 115 (number `110`), where
   `3 Praesent` starts. The bookmarks list the nine chapters `1 Mollit` to `9
   Appendix`, no entry `Contents`. `export-warnings.md` lists 149 entries, as
   in M16; none starts with `TOC_`.
9. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).

After the check, the developer compares the preset notes and `data.json` with
`m17-state-backup/handover/` (checksums) and restores what differs; the preset
`Contents.md` and the export folder stay unless the user asks to remove them.
