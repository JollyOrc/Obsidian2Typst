# M24 — The print checks: low resolution and wide word gaps (US-034)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #131 to #133, and after any change to the image helpers,
the narrow columns or the word-gap scan.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): as M21,
and two more files in the scratch vault: the template
`Obsidian2Typst/templates/Justify.typ` (it justifies the text, as a book's
template does; the plugin itself does not) and the preset
`Obsidian2Typst/presets/Entries justified.md` (the preset `Entries` with that
template). An empty export folder: `spikes/typst-wasm/m21-export/2`.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.14.1` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed.

**Known limitations:** the sample's text is generated nonsense with long
words, so many narrow lines gap (132 warnings); with real text there are far
fewer. The numbers below were measured through the plugin's export path
(2026-11-07, scratch probe `m21probe.test.ts`).

## Steps

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.14.0`. Close the settings. Open the Book section: after `Image
   fit` there is the row `Print resolution` (`Warns about images below this
   dpi; off at 0.`); its gear shows `Minimum print resolution` with `300
   (built-in)`. Close the settings.
2. Choose the preset `Entries justified`. Export `Sample/Menagerie.md` into
   `spikes/typst-wasm/m21-export/2`. The notices read `Compiling…` and
   `Exported with 161 warnings`.
3. `export-warnings.md` lists `TEXT_WIDE_GAP` 132 and `IMAGE_LOW_DPI` 4:
   `"assets/entry-tall.png": 253 dpi at 35.9 mm (min 300)`,
   `"assets/entry-square.png": 152 dpi at 100.0 mm (min 300)`,
   `"assets/entry-slim.png": 264 dpi at 28.9 mm (min 300)`,
   `"assets/entry-small.png": 132 dpi at 52.3 mm (min 300)`.
   Among the gaps: `"Reed Cat": "Ut pariatur nostrud cillum" (562 %)`.
4. Open `Menagerie.pdf` at 100 % or larger, page 8 (`Reed Cat`): find the
   line `Ut pariatur nostrud cillum` in the narrow column; its gaps are
   visibly wide. Compare with a full-width paragraph on the same page: no
   such gaps (SCAR-025).
5. In the style panel, preview `Sample/Menagerie.md` with the same preset.
   The warning count does not include the word gaps (the preview never
   scans): expected 29, the export's 161 less the 132 `TEXT_WIDE_GAP`
   (computed, not measured in the app).
6. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).
