# M32 — A progress tab during an export, and the whole export's time (US-042)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** before G2 of US-042, which plans a progress tab that opens 2 s into an export without taking the focus
(AC-7). Two things are not measured yet (SCAR-024, SCAR-045): what Obsidian does with a tab opened in the background
during an export, and how long a whole export takes inside Obsidian (the Node lower bound is in
`docs/technical/spike-export-time.md`).

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop (the author's default environment: M1 MacBook, macOS 27.0.1, Obsidian 1.15.0). Prepared
by the developer (SCAR-012, SCAR-018) in the scratch vault (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`):

- the **spike build `0.15.3-m32.1`**: 0.15.3 plus `src/plugin/m32-spike.ts` (copy of `spikes/m32/m32-spike.ts`) and
  hooks in `main.ts` and `export-command.ts`; not for release, removed after the check. 2 s after an export starts
  (after the folder is chosen) it opens a tab `Exporting <note>` in the background, with the current step as its
  heading, a counter that ticks every 0.25 s (`(compiling)` while a compile runs) and a bar. It writes what it sees
  (times, the active tab and the focused element before and after the tab opens, when the tab's view is drawn, the
  ticks during the compile) to the console and to the note `M32 log.md` at the vault's root;
- an empty export folder outside the vault: `~/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m32/export`.

The starting state is `spikes/typst-wasm/m32-before.txt` (315 files, all OK before any change, identical to the
state after M31's undo); the `.obsidian` folder is in `spikes/typst-wasm/m32-state-backup/` (14 files, all OK).
Obsidian was not running during the preparation: **start Obsidian; it opens the scratch vault.**

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected version:** `0.16.0-m33.1` (first line of the settings tab, and the plugin list). If it differs, stop.

**Decided before the run (SCAR-032):**

- **A (the tab).** AC-7 stands as written if the log shows, for the book: the tab opened while the note's tab
  stayed the active one and the focused element did not change; the tab's view was drawn (its `onOpen`) within
  0.5 s of opening; at least 4 ticks while a compile ran and no gap between ticks over 1000 ms. If the view is
  drawn only when the tab is clicked (Obsidian defers background tabs), AC-7 is amended before G2 (the tab draws
  the current state when it is shown); if the focus moves, AC-7 is rewritten.
- **B (the time).** Recorded as numbers. The 2 s line of AC-7 holds if every book export takes more than 2 s
  and the short note less than 2 s.

## Steps

1. Start Obsidian. Settings → Obsidian2Typst: the first line says **`Obsidian2Typst 0.15.3-m32.1`**. Close the
   settings.
2. Click the ribbon icon `Obsidian2Typst` (printer) and choose the preset **`A5 print`**.
3. Open **`Sample/Book.md`** from the file explorer and click once into its text (do not type).
4. Command palette: **`Obsidian2Typst: Export current note`**. In the folder dialog choose
   **`~/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m32/export`**. Then **touch nothing** and watch the tab bar: does a tab
   `Exporting Book` appear, and does `Book` stay the tab in front? As soon as the new tab appears, wait about one
   second, then click it: does it show a heading and a counter that keeps moving (with `(compiling)`)? Wait until
   its heading says `Finished (exported)`.
5. Close the `Exporting Book` tab. Open **`Glyph check.md`**, click into its text, run **`Export current note`**
   again into the same folder. Watch: does a tab appear? (Expected: no; the export is short.)
6. Open `Sample/Book.md` again and repeat step 4 once (the compiler is warm now). Close the tab when it says
   `Finished`.
7. Send what you saw in steps 4 to 6 (anything that surprised you), and **quit Obsidian**. The developer reads
   `M32 log.md` and the export folder, and undoes the vault against `m32-before.txt`.

## Results (2026-11-17, build `0.15.3-m32.1`)

The owner: steps 1 to 7 "all went well, no surprises" (the tab appeared with `Book` in front; the counter moved; no
tab for the short note). From `M32 log.md` (times from the moment the folder was chosen):

| Run | Compile | Whole export | Tab | `onOpen` | Ticks in the compile | Longest gap between ticks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Book, 1st | 867 → 8142 ms | **12780 ms** | at 2009 ms | 2005 ms | 24 | **2232 ms** |
| Glyph check | 6 → 287 ms | **381 ms** | none | — | — | — |
| Book, 2nd | 446 → 7783 ms | **12453 ms** | at 2007 ms | 2006 ms | 23 | **2116 ms** |

Export folder: `Book.pdf` (3.9 MB), `Book.typ`, `Glyph check.pdf`, `Glyph check.typ`, `assets/`,
`export-warnings.md` (217 warnings for the book: the sample book's own, as in the report).

- **B holds:** both book exports over 12 s, the short note 0.38 s. One compile each (no `Compiling… (2 of …)`); about
  4.6 s pass between the compile's end and the export's end.
- **A, drawing: holds.** The view was drawn at once (`onOpen` 4 ms after the 2 s mark), not deferred.
- **A, updates during the compile: hold.** 23 to 24 ticks in about 6 s of compile (one every 0.25 s).
- **A, no gap over 1000 ms: fails.** One gap of 2.1 to 2.2 s in each book run. Since the compile ticked steadily, it
  falls after the compile (writing, the word-gap scan, the spreads rewrite or the report: not measured which).
- **A, the focus: fails as logged, holds as seen.** The focused element stayed `body` before and after (the
  palette had left no editor focused, so this item tells nothing), but `getMostRecentLeaf()` named the new tab
  (`o2t-m32-progress`) right after it opened, while the owner saw `Book` stay in front. Obsidian counted the new tab as
  the most recent leaf without showing it; what that means for a command run during the export (which reads the
  active note) is not measured.

**Owner's decision (2026-11-17): accept as is.** Both failed items are taken as harmless (nothing showed in use); AC-7
stays as written; the findings stay recorded here.
