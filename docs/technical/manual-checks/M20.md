# M20 — A layout that settles (US-028 AC-14 to AC-17, #116)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #117 to #119 (the hints and the choice of the next compile,
Typst's warnings read back, the export loop and the fallback), and after any
change to the image fit helper, the compile path or the export run.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): the test
build is installed in the scratch vault (`npm run install:scratch`; `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the fresh build, `main.js`
sha256 `83fa076406cb…`), with the sample vault as `Sample/` (73 files). New
fixtures: the note `Settle check.md` (the generated note of US-028 AC-18,
seed 3, 60 images, as `![[settle-map.png]]`, `![[settle-wide.png]]`,
`![[settle-tall.png]]`), its three images in the folder `Settle check/`, and
the preset note `Obsidian2Typst/presets/Settle check.md` (group `Print`; A5,
facing pages, `images.minScale: 70`). Each of these names exists once in the
vault (listing, 2026-11-03). The preset `Image fit` (from M18) is used as it
is. The plugin data (`data.json`: active preset `Book parts`, strict mode
off; the Defaults set folios and the running header at level 4, body 12 pt,
headings 18 pt) is not changed. The plugin files, `data.json` and every
preset note are backed up in `spikes/typst-wasm/m20-state-backup/before/`
(23 files) and, as handed over, in `handover/`, with checksums. Two empty
export folders: `spikes/typst-wasm/m20-export/1` and `…/2`.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.15.0` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian once
so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations:** your own book is not part of this check (your decision
of 2026-11-03; it is checked after the release). The fallback to
`images.minScale: 100` (`IMAGE_FIT_OFF`) cannot be produced in the app: no
note measured needs more than 7 compiles, and the limit of 8 cannot be
lowered there; automated tests cover it. The preview compiles once, so for
`Settle check.md` it counts a layout that did not settle while the export
does not (story decision C). The notices of an export disappear after a few
seconds; watch the top right corner while it runs.

The numbers below were measured from the files as installed (the presets
folder, `data.json`, the notes) through the plugin's own `convertNote` and
`runExport` with the bundled compiler (2026-11-03, scratch probe
`m120/measure.test.ts`).

## Steps

Keep the settings tab closed and the style panel open from step 2 on.

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.14.0`. Close the settings.
2. In the ribbon menu (printer icon) choose the preset `Settle check` (under
   `Print`). Open `Settle check.md` and run `O2T: Export to PDF`; choose the
   folder `spikes/typst-wasm/m20-export/1`. While it runs, the notices read
   `Compiling…`, then `Compiling… (2 of up to 8)`, `Compiling… (3 of up to
   8)` and `Compiling… (4 of up to 8)` — four compiles, no fifth — and at the
   end `Exported with 18 warnings`.
3. Open `export-warnings.md` in that folder: `IMAGE_MOVED` 10 and
   `IMAGE_FLOATED` 8; no `LAYOUT_NOT_SETTLED` and no `IMAGE_FIT_OFF`.
4. Open `Settle check.pdf`: 49 pages. Page 1 starts with `1 Chapter 1` and
   `S0 0 lorem ipsum`. The last page has the folio `49`, the header `Chapter
   6` and the text `End 0 lorem ipsum …`. Every page carries an image (solid
   rust-orange boxes of three sizes). Look at two pages at reading size (100 %), one
   with an image at the top of the page (a floated image) and one with a gap
   before an image (a moved one): the text before and after each image is
   complete, nothing overlaps (SCAR-025).
5. Open `Settle check.typ` in a text editor. Near the top stand the lines
   `#let o2t-fit-hints = ("o2t-fit-1": (d: "full", r: 1.0), …` (one entry per
   image, 60) and `#let o2t-fit-pinned = false`.
6. In the style panel, with `Settle check.md` still open, press Refresh. The
   status line reads `Preview of: Settle check · 24 warnings` (the preview's
   one compile: 23 image warnings and `LAYOUT_NOT_SETTLED`).
7. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Image fit`. Open `Sample/Book.md`
   and export it to `spikes/typst-wasm/m20-export/2`. The notices read
   `Compiling…` once — no `(2 of up to 8)` — and `Exported with 149
   warnings`. `Book.pdf` has 424 pages, and `Book.typ` holds the line
   `#let o2t-fit-hints = (:)`.
8. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report
   it even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).

After the check, the developer compares the plugin files, `data.json` and
the preset notes with `m20-state-backup/handover/` (checksums, Obsidian
closed) and restores what differs; `Settle check.md`, its images and its
preset stay unless the user asks to remove them.
