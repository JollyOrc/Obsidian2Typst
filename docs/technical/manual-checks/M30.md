# M30 — The ◆ marks of the statblock band, in Obsidian's preview and in the PDF (US-041, #170)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #171 and #172 (US-041), and after any change to the statblock's
band in the Typst helper (`o2t-sb-box`), to the preview's `draw`, or to the band's
rules in `styles.css`.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop and a PDF viewer. Prepared by the developer
(SCAR-012, SCAR-018): the test build installed in the scratch vault (`npm run
install:scratch`; `main.js`, `manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the
fresh build; the main.js checksum is in the hand-over note) and the note `Band
check.md` at the vault's root: a chapter `Menagerie` with five entries whose names
are invented, each with a statblock (`vigour`, three stats, two attacks) and a
short text, each on its own page; the presets `Entries band 8` and `Entries band 16` (step 7). Empty export folders:
`spikes/typst-wasm/m30-export/1`, `/2` and `/3`. The vault's
state before the install is backed up (a checksum list of 315 files and the four
files the install changes) in `spikes/typst-wasm/m30-state-backup/`. Part B uses
your own vault; nothing there is prepared or changed by the developer.

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`) for part A, and in
your own vault for part B. **Expected version:** `0.17.0` (first line of the
settings tab, also the plugin list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is
installed. Restart Obsidian once so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations (SCAR-024, SCAR-032, SCAR-045):** the developer measured in
Node (2026-11-16, Typst 0.14.2, the bundled Libertinus Serif at 11.5 pt, the
preset `Entries`: an A5 text width of 105 mm, a statblock of 57.75 mm and a band
of 53.75 mm) what the PDF draws, listed under step 5. **Not measured:** the
preview in Obsidian (its font, its pane width, Chromium's flex layout: the fake
DOM of the tests has no layout) and your own preset's font (part B). The preview
may wrap a name at other places than the PDF does, because the fonts differ; that
is not a defect, the marks must be level with the middle of whatever lines result.
In the preview a word too wide to fit beside the marks keeps the marks (they wrap
with the name): a decision of the story (D3), not a defect.

Read every picture at reading size (100 %) and look closely at the band at 400 %
(SCAR-025).

## Steps

### A — the scratch vault

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.15.2-m30.1`. Close the settings.
2. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Entries`. Open `Band check.md` in
   **reading view** (not live preview), with the pane at its normal width.
3. The preview, at its normal width. **Expected:** five statblocks. `Fen Hare`:
   the band `◆ Fen Hare ◆` on one line, the marks next to the name (as before). For
   each other name, if the name still fits on one line the band looks the same;
   the longest name (`The Ancient Marsh Dragon … Whispering Reeds`) wraps over two
   or more lines: **a ◆ at the left end and a ◆ at the right end of the band, each
   level with the middle of the lines**, the lines centred between them (not a mark
   at the start of the first line and one at the end of the last line). Report for
   each statblock whether it wrapped and how the marks sit.
4. Narrow the pane (drag the divider, or open the note in a narrow split) until the
   statblocks are about 6 cm wide. **Expected:** `Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey
   Reaches` and the next two names wrap with a mark at each end, level with the
   middle; `Pneumonoultramicroscopically` is a word that cannot fit beside the
   marks: **the preview keeps the marks and wraps them with the name** (they may
   stand above or below it); report how that looks. Widen the pane again.
5. Run `O2T: Export to PDF` into `spikes/typst-wasm/m30-export/1` and open the PDF.
   On the five entry pages: **expected** (rehearsal in Node, the band is 53.75 mm
   wide):
   1. `Fen Hare`: one line, `◆ Fen Hare ◆`, the marks one space from the name.
   2. `Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey Reaches`: two lines `Old Marsh Dragon of the` /
      `Grey Reaches`, a ◆ at the left end and a ◆ at the right end of the band,
      each at the height between the two lines.
   3. `The Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey Reaches and the Giant Swamp Chieftain`:
      three lines (`The Old Marsh Dragon of` / `the Grey Reaches and the` / `Giant
      Swamp Chieftain`), the marks level with the middle line.
   4. `Pneumonoultramicroscopically`: **one line and no marks** (the word is too
      wide to fit beside them).
   5. `The Ancient Marsh Dragon … Whispering Reeds`: four lines (`The Ancient Marsh
      Dragon` / `of the Grey Reaches and` / `the Giant Swamp Chieftain` / `of the
      Whispering Reeds`), the marks level with the middle, between lines two and
      three.
   Every band is centred; the rule under the band and the rest of each statblock
   are as before; the text beside the statblock follows it as in 0.15.1.
6. Look at the band of entry 2 at 400 %: the marks are clean ◆ shapes, not
   touching the text, centred on the gap between the lines.
7. **A smaller and a larger band size.** The band's font size is a field of the
   *preset note* (`entries.statblock.sizes.band`), not of the settings tab; the
   developer has made two copies of `Entries` that differ only in that field:
   `Entries band 8` (8 pt) and `Entries band 16` (16 pt), in
   `Obsidian2Typst/presets/`. In the ribbon menu choose `Entries band 8`, export
   `Band check.md` into `spikes/typst-wasm/m30-export/2` and open the PDF.
   **Expected** (Node rehearsal, band 53.75 mm; the marks are at the band's ends,
   level with the middle, whenever there is a mark):
   1. `Fen Hare`: one line, marks beside the name.
   2. `Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey Reaches`: **one line** with its marks (the
      smaller type fits).
   3. `The Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey Reaches and the Giant Swamp Chieftain`: two
      lines (`The Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey` / `Reaches and the Giant Swamp
      Chieftain`), a mark at each end between them.
   4. `Pneumonoultramicroscopically`: **one line with its marks** (at 8 pt the word
      fits beside them).
   5. The longest name: three lines, the marks level with the second line.
   Then choose `Entries band 16`, export into `spikes/typst-wasm/m30-export/3`.
   **Expected:** the type is larger and the names wrap more: `Fen Hare` one line
   with marks; `Old Marsh Dragon of the Grey Reaches` three lines (`Old Marsh
   Dragon` / `of the Grey` / `Reaches`); the three-line name of step 5 takes five
   lines; the longest name seven; in each the marks stand at the ends of the band
   level with the middle line. `Pneumonoultramicroscopically` has **no marks** and
   is wider than the band: the word is not broken and runs over the statblock's
   frame (as in 0.15.1; a decision of the story, AC-4). Also look at the
   preview with each preset: the band's type follows the preset; report how the
   wrapped names look there.
8. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report it
   even if it seems unrelated (SCAR-015).
