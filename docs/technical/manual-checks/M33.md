# M33 — The export dialog, the progress tab, the notices and the settings tab's new rows (US-042)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after T1 to T6 of US-042 (#175 to #180), before the release 0.16.0; ticket #181 (T7), AC-15. What the
tests cannot show is checked here (SCAR-024, SCAR-045): the dialog's keys and lines in Obsidian, the progress tab
opened in the background and reused, Cancel during a real compile, the notices as they appear, the clipboard, the
list of enabled plugins, the gear that opens this plugin's settings tab (`app.setting`, private), and the title of a
reused tab (`leaf.updateHeader`, not in Obsidian's typings).

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop (the author's default environment: M1 MacBook, macOS 27.0.1, Obsidian 1.15.0). Prepared
by the developer (SCAR-012, SCAR-018) in the scratch vault (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`):

- the **test build `0.16.0-m33.1`** of branch `us-042-ui-standard` (T6 `1dac8dc` plus the version), installed by
  `npm run install:scratch`;
- an empty export folder outside the vault: `~/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m33/export` (typed into the dialog
  as an absolute path, your home folder written out: `/Users/<you>/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m33/export`);
- the preset **`Minimal`**, which has no remembered export folder (the dialog case first, then the remembered one);
- the starting state: `spikes/m33/m33-before.txt` (checksums of every file of the vault) and a full copy of the
  vault in `spikes/m33/vault-before/`, both made while the scratch vault was not open. The checked actions write the
  plugin's `data.json`, `.obsidian/workspace.json` and the warnings notes `Glyph check export warnings.md` (new) and
  `Book export warnings.md` (rewritten) at the vault's root; the full copy holds them all.

Obsidian may stay open with your other vaults: **open the scratch vault from the vault switcher** (the vault icon at
the bottom left → `Open another vault` → `Open folder as vault`, or pick `scratch-vault` from the list).

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected version:** `0.16.0` (first line
of the settings tab, and the plugin list). If it differs, stop.

**Decided before the run (SCAR-032):** each step names what is expected. The check passes when every step shows it.
A difference is written down with its step number and what was seen, and nothing is changed during the run; after
it, each difference is a bug (a ticket, fixed and re-checked before the release) or a wording or design point the
owner decides. A step that cannot be done is recorded as not run, never as passed. The two findings of M32 (a gap
of about 2 s after the compile; the new tab counted as the most recent leaf) were accepted and are not findings
here.

## Steps

### A. The settings tab (AC-12, AC-13)

1. Settings → `Obsidian2Typst`. The first line reads **`Obsidian2Typst 0.16.0-m33.1`**, with a **bug icon** at its
   right (tooltip `Copy debug info`). There is no heading with the plugin's name.
2. Click the bug icon. Expected: the notice **`Debug info copied.`** Paste the clipboard into any text editor
   outside the vault and save it as `~/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m33/debug-info.txt`.
   Expected (the developer compares the file): the lines `Obsidian2Typst 0.16.0-m33.1`, `Obsidian <version>`,
   `Enabled plugins:`, one line `- obsidian2typst 0.16.0-m33.1`, `Settings file:` and then the text of the plugin's
   `data.json`.
3. Open the section **Page**. Its last row is the button **`Reset this section to defaults`**. Click it. Expected:
   a question `Reset "Page" to its defaults?` with the line `Your values in this section will be removed; presets
   that do not set them use the built-in values.` and the buttons **`Cancel`** (first, with the focus ring) and
   `Reset`. Press **Enter**. Expected: the question closes and nothing changes (`Page numbers` still on).
4. Click the button again and click **`Reset`**. Expected: the tab is drawn again; in *Page*, `Page numbers` and
   `Running header` now show their built-in values; in *Text*, `Body size (pt)` still shows **12**.
5. Open **Book** and click its reset button. Expected: no question (Book has no values of yours), nothing changes.
   *Presets* and *Export* have no reset button. Close the settings.

### B. The export dialog, without a remembered folder (AC-1 to AC-6, AC-11)

6. Click the ribbon icon `Obsidian2Typst` (printer). Expected: the first item is **`Export…`**, then `Export
   current note`, `Open style panel`, then the presets. Choose the preset **`Minimal`**.
7. Open **`Glyph check.md`** and click into its text. Command palette: **`Obsidian2Typst: Export current note`**.
   Expected: `Minimal` has no remembered folder, so the dialog opens: title **`Export Glyph check`**, the preset
   dropdown on `Minimal`, an empty folder field, the red line **`Choose an export folder.`**, `Export` disabled.
8. Type `spikes/m33` into the field. Expected: the red line **`Export folder "spikes/m33" is not an absolute
   path.`**, `Export` disabled; Enter does nothing. Press **Escape**: the dialog closes, nothing is exported.
9. Ribbon icon → **`Export…`**. Expected: the dialog again, the field empty (nothing was saved at step 8). Type
   the export folder as an absolute path (`/Users/<you>/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m33/export`). Expected: no
   red line; the grey line
   **`Glyph check.pdf will be written.`**
10. Change the preset dropdown to **`A5 print`**. Expected: the field changes at once to `/Users/<you>/Desktop`
    (that preset's remembered folder) and the grey line follows. Change it back to **`Minimal`**. Expected: the
    field is empty again. Type the export folder from step 9 again.
11. Click the gear of **`More options`**. Expected: the settings open on the `Obsidian2Typst` tab. Note what
    happened to the dialog, close the settings, and if the dialog is gone, open it again with the ribbon's
    `Export…` and type the folder again.
12. Press **Enter** in the field. Expected: the export runs; **no** progress tab (a short note); the notice
    **`✅ Exported Glyph check.pdf to /Users/<you>/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/m33/export`** (with warnings,
    `, N warning(s) (see export-warnings.md)`), shown for about 5 s.

### C. With the remembered folder, all entry points (AC-1, AC-2)

13. Command palette: **`Obsidian2Typst: Export current note`**. Expected: no dialog; the same notice.
14. Ribbon icon → **`Export current note`**. Expected: no dialog; the same notice.
15. Ribbon icon → **`Export…`**. Expected: the dialog with the folder filled in and the grey line **`Glyph
    check.pdf exists (from <today> <time of step 14>) and will be replaced.`** Press **Escape**.
16. Command palette: **`Obsidian2Typst: Export…`**. Expected: the same dialog. Close it with the **X**.
17. File explorer: right-click `Glyph check.md` → **`Export to PDF`**. Expected: no dialog; the notice.
18. In the note, open its menu (the `⋯` at the top right, or right-click in the text) → **`Export to PDF`**.
    Expected: no dialog; the notice.

### D. The progress tab on the sample book (AC-7 to AC-11)

19. Open **`Sample/Book.md`** and click into its text. Command palette: **`Export current note`**. Expected: no
    dialog (Minimal remembers the folder); about 2 s later a tab **`Exporting Book`** appears **behind** `Book`,
    which stays in front. Click the new tab. Expected: the current step as its heading (`Converting`,
    `Compiling`, …), a moving bar, a **`Cancel`** button; warnings, if any, in a list under the bar, each with its
    code in bold and a yellow left border. Wait for the end. Expected: the heading **`Exported`**, the button
    **`Close`**, and the notice `✅ Exported Book.pdf to …` (with its warnings count, if any).
20. Click `Book` again (leave the export tab open) and run **`Export current note`** once more. Expected: **no
    second tab** — the open one is reused; its title still reads `Exporting Book`. Click it. As soon as its heading
    says **`Compiling`**, click **`Cancel`**. Expected: the notice **`❌ Export cancelled.`**, the heading
    **`Export cancelled`**, the button **`Close`**. Click **`Close`**: the tab closes.

### E. A preset change in the dialog, strict mode, no open note (AC-6, AC-11)

21. Open `Glyph check.md`. Ribbon icon → `Export…`. Change the preset to **`A4 digital`**, type the export folder
    from step 9, press **Enter**. Expected: the export runs and the notice appears. Then open the ribbon menu:
    **`A4 digital`** is now the checked (active) preset. Choose **`Minimal`** again.
22. Settings → `Obsidian2Typst` → *Export* → turn **`Strict mode`** on; close the settings. Open `Sample/Book.md`
    and run **`Export current note`**. Expected: if the book has warnings, the notice **`❌ Export aborted: N
    warnings (strict mode)`** followed by up to five lines `line …: CODE`; the tab, if it opened, says **`Export
    aborted (strict mode)`**. If the book has no warnings, it exports as at step 19; write down which. Turn
    `Strict mode` off again and close the tab if it is open.
23. Close every note tab, so that no note is open. Ribbon icon → **`Export…`**. Expected: the notice **`Open a note
    to export it.`** and no dialog.
24. Write down anything that surprised you, then **close the scratch vault's window** (your other vaults may stay
    open). The developer reads the export folder and `debug-info.txt`, and undoes the vault against
    `m33-before.txt`.
