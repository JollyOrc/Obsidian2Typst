Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after #140 to #144 (epic #139, 0.13.0), and after any change to
the wrap around the statblock, the illustration slots or the quick
reference.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012): the test
build installed in the scratch vault (`npm run install:scratch`; `main.js`,
`manifest.json`, `styles.css` byte-identical to the fresh build; checksums in

vault's state before the install is backed up with a checksum list in the

**Run in the scratch vault** (`spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`). **Expected
version:** `0.14.0-m27.1` (first line of the settings tab, also the plugin
list). If it differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. Restart Obsidian
once so the new build is loaded.

**Known limitations:** the sample's text is generated nonsense. The page
numbers below were measured from the same notes and preset through the
plugin's conversion and the bundled compiler in Node (2026-11-09, the
vault's Defaults: header level 4, body 12 pt), not through Obsidian's own
export; if a page number differs by one, look one page further and report
it. A US-030 decision is still open for the user (a list at the statblock's
edge is not split; see the report): no step depends on it.

Read every page at reading size (100 %, SCAR-025) and zoom in on the edge of
the column beside each statblock.

## Steps

1. Open Settings → Obsidian2Typst. The first line reads `Obsidian2Typst
   0.14.0-m27.1`. Close the settings.
2. In the ribbon menu choose the preset `Entries`. Open `Sample/Menagerie.md`
   and run `O2T: Export to PDF` into `spikes/typst-wasm/m25-export/1`. Note
   the number of warnings in the notice (not a pass criterion).
3. Open `Menagerie.pdf`: 47 pages.
4. **The wrap (page 2, `Fen Hare`).** The statblock stands at the top right.
   The opening paragraph fills the column on its left down to the
   statblock's lower edge (its last line in the column ends near the
   column's right edge, not with a hyphen); the same paragraph then
   continues at full width directly below the statblock, starting at the
   left margin, not indented, with the normal space between paragraphs
   above it. No text runs into the statblock, none is missing or doubled
   at the cut (read the last words in the column and the first words below
   it together: they make one sentence). Before 0.13.0 the paragraph stayed
   in the narrow column to its end.
5. **The column filled with more than one block (page 30, `Gorse Imp`).**
   The short first paragraph (`Small and quick and mean.` …) stands in the
   column, and the next paragraph starts there too and continues below the
   statblock at full width.
6. **A nested entry (page 35, `Rime Kit` inside `Rime Sprite`).** Its own
   statblock has its text wrapped around it in the same way, inside the
   parent's text.
7. **The landscape picture under the text (pages 6–7, `Cave Wyrm`).** On the
   right page the long picture follows the text with a small gap: it is at
   full text width and centred in the space left below the text (as much
   room above it as below), not pushed to the page's foot.
8. **Today's rule kept (pages 4–5, `Marsh Owl`).** Its text leaves too little
   room under it on page 4, so the picture is on page 5 at its smaller size
   with its credit `Drawn by A. Nobody`, and page 5's header reads `Marsh
   Owl (continued)`.
9. **Portrait pictures in columns, as before (page 8 `Reed Cat`, page 25
   `Mire Toad`, page 28 `Ash Moth`).** The text is in a narrow column beside
   a tall picture on the outer edge (left on a left page, right on a right
   page): these entries have too much text for the stacked form.
10. **The quick reference (page 38).** The running header reads `Quick
    Reference` (before 0.13.0: `Fen Hare` in this vault). The columns are
    `Entry`, `Vigour` (with a capital V, not `vigour`), `Might`, `Wits`,
    `Speed`, `Track`, `Page`; there is no `Section` column. A row with the
    section's name in bold spans the table before each section's entries:
    `Beasts` before `Fen Hare`, then `Horrors`, then `Spirits`. Click `Fen
    Hare` in the Entry column: the PDF viewer jumps to page 2. The name has
    no page number after it (the Page column has it).
11. **The sheet page (page 39).** Its header reads `Encounter Sheet`.
12. **The second chapter (pages 44 and 45).** The `Relics` quick reference's
    header reads `Quick Reference`, with a section row `Rings`; the sheet
    page's header `Encounter Sheet`.
13. Open `Layout check.md` (vault root) and export it with the preset
    `Entries` into `spikes/typst-wasm/m25-export/2`. Open `Layout
    check.pdf`: 13 pages.
14. **A long first paragraph (page 2, `Long Hare`, 170 words).** About 22
    words stand in the column; the other words run at full width below the
    statblock; the paragraph is printed whole and ends on page 2.
15. **A square picture grown (pages 4–5, `Square Owl`).** All its text is on
    the left page. The right page shows the picture 100 × 100 mm (the full
    text width; before 0.13.0 at most half the text height) with its credit
    under it, the two together centred between the top and the foot of the
    text area.
16. **A tall picture with no text left (pages 6–7, `Tall Cat`).** The right
    page shows the picture at the full text height (about 80 × 165 mm),
    centred left to right — not in a narrow column on the outer edge.
17. **A very slim picture with no text left (pages 8–9, `Slim Eel`).** The
    picture (about 41 × 165 mm) is centred left to right, not at the outer
    edge (both forms give it the same size; the centred one is used).
18. **Stacked with text (page 10, `Short Moth`, a page-sized entry).** Below
    the statblock and the text beside it, the last short paragraph runs at
    full width; the picture follows below it, about 85 × 104 mm, centred
    left to right, reaching down to the foot of the text area.
19. Page 12 (the quick reference) has the header `Quick Reference` and a
    section row `Checks`; page 13 the header `Encounter Sheet`.
20. Anything else that looks odd (a gap, an overlap, text cut off, a
    picture cropped, a wrong header), on any page of either file: note the
    page and what you see.
